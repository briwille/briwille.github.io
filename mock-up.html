Sports Campaign

Minnesota Wild Theme Night

View Case Study

For this project, I created a full theme night concept for the Minnesota Wild called “State of Speed,” an automotive-inspired game night designed to increase attendance, fan engagement, sponsorship opportunities, and revenue. My role in this project was developing the entire campaign concept and creating all of the mockups featured throughout the presentation.

The event combined hockey culture with Minnesota’s strong automotive community through interactive experiences like Toyota vehicle showcases, racing-themed arena visuals, driving simulators, themed merchandise, fan contests, and social media activations using #StateofSpeed.
